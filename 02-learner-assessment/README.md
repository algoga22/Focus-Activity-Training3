# Challenge 2 – Learner assessment

Diagnostic quiz measuring what participants already know, their misconceptions and what they can do, against ALL learning objectives. Max 5 minutes. Must work online and onsite. Run it again at the end to measure progress.

## Simple examples

Questions based on real situations, easy to score (good / partial / wrong):

**1. You open an attachment and your screen shows: "Your files are locked. Pay to get them back." What do you do first?**
- a) Pay quickly so the work is not lost
- b) Turn off the Wi-Fi / unplug the cable and tell your coordinator ✅
- c) Delete the message and carry on working

**2. You sent a list of beneficiaries' names and addresses to the wrong email address. What should you do?**
- a) Nothing, it was an accident
- b) Tell your coordinator straight away, even if it is embarrassing ✅
- c) Send a second email asking the person to delete it, and tell nobody

**3. Which of these is a security incident?** (several answers possible)
- Lost work phone ✅
- A colleague can't remember their password, but it hasn't been used by anyone else
- Strange emails sent from your account that you didn't write ✅
