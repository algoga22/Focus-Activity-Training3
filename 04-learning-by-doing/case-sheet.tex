{\small\textbf{Food for All} is a small food bank: 2 employees, 40 volunteers, no IT department.
\textbf{Sofia} is a volunteer and the treasurer: she pays the suppliers from the NGO's online bank account.\par}
\vspace{3mm}

\moment{1}{TUESDAY, 16:40}{An email arrives. \textbf{From:} Paul Martin, President \textless paul.martin.foodforall@gmail.com\textgreater\\
\emph{``Hi Sofia, I'm in a meeting with a big donor and can't talk. Please pay today the €2,000 invoice of our new supplier
(bank details attached). Keep it confidential until I announce the partnership. Thanks! Paul''}\\[1mm]
At 16:55 Sofia pays. At 17:10 she reads the email again: Paul always writes from an \textbf{@foodforall.org} address.}
\question{1}{Is this an incident? What should Sofia do in the first minutes?}{3}

\moment{2}{17:15}{Sofia calls Paul on his usual phone number: \textbf{he never sent that email}. The coordinator, Lea, is on holiday until Monday.
Sofia feels terrible: ``€2,000 of the donors' money, because of me.'' A colleague says:
\emph{``Reply to the email and ask them to send the money back. And let's keep this between us.''}}
\question{2}{Who should Sofia tell, how, and how fast?}{3}
\question{3}{What should Sofia NOT do?}{2}

\moment{3}{17:40}{The bank's fraud team calls back. To try to stop the payment, they need precise information.}
\question{4}{What should Sofia have written down?}{2}
