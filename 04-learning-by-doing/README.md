# Challenge 4 – Learning by doing

**In one sentence:** a hands-on exercise where participants practise **one key skill** themselves, in a realistic NGO situation, right after learning it.

## Purpose
- **Practise ONE critical skill**, the most important one of the training. It is not a review of everything.
- Participants **find the solution themselves** (deciding, analysing, solving) instead of listening.
- **Build autonomy:** after the exercise they can do it alone in real life.

## When in the session
**26–38 min (12 minutes)**, right after the theory on the first steps (8' group work + 4' debrief).

## Requirements
- **Not a game.** Use a group debate, an exercise or a case analysis.
- Focuses on **one** learning objective, chosen carefully because this type of activity takes time to prepare and to run.
- **Realistic NGO scenario** (real constraints: small team, no IT department, volunteers, sensitive beneficiary data…).
- **Clear instructions:** scenario, steps, expected result and guidance for the trainer.
- **Debrief:** review answers and mistakes together and explain the correct behaviour.
- Feasible in the time with simple materials.

## Simple examples

**Case analysis: "Friday afternoon at the food bank"**
> It's Friday at 17:00. Marie, a volunteer, opens an email "Invoice overdue" and clicks the attachment. Her screen goes black with a message asking for €500 in Bitcoin. The coordinator has already left and there is no IT person.

In groups, participants fill in a simple sheet:
1. What is the first thing Marie should do?
2. Who should she tell, and how, if the coordinator isn't there?
3. What should she write down?
4. What should she **not** do?

**Debrief:** each group shares its answers, the trainer shows the correct steps and explains the common mistakes (paying, turning off the computer, hiding it until Monday).

**Other possible cases:**
- A work phone with beneficiaries' contacts is lost on the bus.
- The NGO's Facebook page starts posting strange messages.
