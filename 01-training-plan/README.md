# Challenge 1 – Training plan

The skeleton of our 60-minute session **"When something goes wrong: a simple incident response"**, with the three columns asked for (**Topic – What?**, **Duration – How long?**, **Description – How?**), plus the learning objectives covered and the challenge each sequence belongs to.

PDF version (one page, for the trainers): [`Training3_Session_Plan.pdf`](Training3_Session_Plan.pdf), generated by [`tools/build_session_plan.py`](../tools/build_session_plan.py).

## Learning objectives

Written from the title and description of Training 3. By the end of the training, participants are able to:

| # | Objective |
|---|---|
| LO1 | **Recognise** a security incident |
| LO2 | **Take the first steps:** stop and disconnect |
| LO3 | **Report** the incident quickly to the right person |
| LO4 | **Write down** what happened |
| LO5 | **Avoid** the common mistakes (paying, deleting, hiding) |

The same five objectives are used everywhere: the quiz has one question per objective, the game one phase per objective (plus the trap cards for LO5), the case one question per step, and the cheat sheet one section per step.

## Why the plan is built this way

- **Every topic starts with an action verb** and says what participants will be able to do (recognise, apply, play, measure).
- **Theory and practice alternate:** about 37 of the 60 minutes are activities (the quiz twice, the "Incident or not?" vote, the game, the case). No explanation lasts more than 8 minutes, and each one has an interaction (raise your hand, thumbs up or down, saying the rule aloud).
- **One thread runs through the hour:** *an incident is like a fire* (the sooner you act, the less damage) and *one rule, four steps* (Stop, Disconnect, Report, Write down). The misconceptions found by the quiz are answered in the slides and come back as trap cards in the game.
- **Realistic timing:** participants scan the QR code during the introductions, so no time is lost connecting. They join once and stay in the same room for the quiz, the game and the final quiz.
- **Learning is measured:** the same quiz at the start (minute 5) and at the end (minute 53), compared automatically by the web app per objective and per person. The game (minute 26) and the case (minute 41) give evidence in between.
- **No prerequisites:** no technical words without an explanation, everyday examples, no account to create. It works online (link in the chat, breakout rooms) and onsite (QR code, paper backups).
