# Evaluation criteria

Each deliverable is worth 100 points (600 in total). Source: bootcamp briefing (not stored in this repository).

## Challenge 1 – Training plan
| Criterion | Pts |
|---|---|
| Wording of topics: each point describes what participants will learn or do | 20 |
| Consistency with the online training description: all points covered | 20 |
| Suitability for target audience: no extra prerequisites | 5 |
| Description of sequences: exercise, demo, discussion… | 20 |
| Variety and balance of methods (theory / practice) | 15 |
| Realistic durations | 15 |
| Total duration matches the Trainer Information Form | 5 |

## Challenge 2 – Learner assessment
| Criterion | Pts |
|---|---|
| Relevance to training objectives (5 pts per objective) | 25 |
| Quality and clarity of questions (4 pts per question) | 20 |
| Quality of answers: indisputable correct answer, plausible distractors, no tricks | 5 |
| Ability to measure progress (works before and after training) | 25 |
| Tool choice: justified (3 pts per valid, factual reason) | 20 |
| Coverage and balance across topics | 5 |

## Challenge 3 – Serious game
| Criterion | Pts |
|---|---|
| Players apply the exact knowledge and skills of the learning objectives | 20 |
| Clear, observable evidence that learning outcomes are achieved | 20 |
| Originality and creativity of the concept | 20 |
| Clear rules, minimal setup, playable within the time | 20 |
| Objective indicators of understanding; misconceptions easy to spot | 20 |

## Challenge 4 – Learning by doing
| Criterion | Pts |
|---|---|
| Core learning outcome relevance (critical skill for NGO security) | 20 |
| Objective–exercise alignment | 20 |
| Hands-on and interactive | 15 |
| Relevance to NGO environments | 15 |
| Feasibility in one session | 10 |
| Design and instructions (scenario, steps, expected output, trainer guidance) | 10 |
| Feedback and debrief | 10 |

## Challenge 5 – Take-home
| Criterion | Pts |
|---|---|
| Topic importance and focus | 15 |
| Essential information and accuracy | 20 |
| Actionability and decision support | 20 |
| Prevention of common mistakes | 10 |
| Real-life and NGO relevance | 15 |
| Usability and visual hierarchy | 10 |
| Take-home value and durability | 10 |

## Slides
| Criterion | Pts |
|---|---|
| Visual communication | 20 |
| Balanced and consistent use of colour | 20 |
| Short, action-oriented wording | 20 |
| One slide, one idea | 20 |
| Purposeful use of animations and transitions | 20 |
