# Challenge 5 – Take-home cheat sheet

A one-page, visual job aid: clear triggers, steps and decisions, common mistakes to avoid, realistic NGO situations. Only content covered during the training.

## Simple example

A one-page sheet to stick next to the computer:

> **🚨 Something seems wrong? Follow these 4 steps**
>
> 1. **STOP:** don't click anything else
> 2. **DISCONNECT:** turn off the Wi-Fi or unplug the cable (don't switch the computer off)
> 3. **REPORT:** call ______________ (name / phone). If there's no answer, call ______________
> 4. **WRITE DOWN:** what happened, when, and what you saw (take a photo of the screen)
>
> **❌ Never:** pay a ransom · delete emails or files · hide it out of embarrassment
>
> **When to use it:** strange message on screen, lost phone or laptop, email sent to the wrong person, your account sending messages you didn't write.
